    Key Local Assistance & In-Person Help Points

    🏛️
    District & Block Agriculture Offices

    Contact the District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or Block Development Office (BDO) for cluster enrollments and scheme guidelines.

    🔬
    Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)

    Local agricultural science centers providing technical training, demonstrations, and crop management orientation.

    🧪
    Soil Testing Laboratories (STLs)

    District and block labs for soil sample submission, testing, and soil card interpretation.

    🤝
    Village Extension (Kisan Mitra / Gram Sevak)

    Village-level agriculture assistants who provide in-person guidance for farmers without internet access.
